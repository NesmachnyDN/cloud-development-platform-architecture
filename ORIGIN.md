# Origin and publication boundary

## Origin

This architecture case was independently authored in 2024 as a graduation project for an Enterprise Architecture / TOGAF 10 training course. The original work was developed incrementally across a sequence of architecture assignments and later consolidated into a graduation presentation and ArchiMate model.

The public repository is not a copy of the original course repository. It is a curated portfolio edition organized around the architecture transformation storyline rather than around assignment numbers.

## What is retained

The public edition retains the author's architecture reasoning covering:

- motivation and measurable outcomes;
- capability modeling;
- AS-IS and TO-BE value streams;
- baseline and target architecture;
- organizational transformation;
- gap analysis;
- risk and change-readiness assessment;
- implementation and migration planning.

## What is intentionally excluded

The following material is not copied into the public repository:

- the original `.git` directory and commit history;
- historical remote URLs and author email metadata;
- course/homework instructions and submission scaffolding;
- raw working spreadsheets and presentation source files;
- model objects that existed only to track completion of course assignments;
- any credentials or environment-specific configuration.

## Product names

The original target-state model used publicly available commercial products as illustrative Solution Building Blocks. Those product choices are treated as replaceable SBBs in the public case. Their presence in the source study does not imply affiliation, endorsement, or disclosure of proprietary vendor information.

## Authorship

The enterprise-architecture analysis, modeling, transformation design and original ArchiMate model represented by this repository were authored by Dmitry Nesmachny. The public edition reorganizes and sanitizes that material for portfolio presentation.
